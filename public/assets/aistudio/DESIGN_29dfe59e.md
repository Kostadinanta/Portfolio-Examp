## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a synthesis of **Swiss-Editorial Bento architecture**, **Meridial dark monochrome minimalism**, and **prismatic aura light refraction**. Designed for an engineer and interface architect, the visual language balances surgical precision with organic optical beauty.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intent
- **Surgical Precision & Rigor:** Structural layout rules, technical indices (`(01) // ABOUT`), live system clocks, and monospaced telemetry data communicate deep engineering acumen and methodical execution.
- **Atmospheric Sophistication:** Deep obsidian canvases (`#0B0C0E`) intersected by spectral gradients (violet-to-coral light sweeps) evoke tactile depth, digital luminescence, and modern high-end software craftsmanship.
- **Editorial Wit:** High-impact geometric grotesk typography paired with delicate italicized serif accents delivers editorial rhythm, breaking traditional portfolio clichés with bespoke typographic tension.

### Design Movement & Language
The design style mixes **Swiss International Typographic Style**, **Glassmorphism**, and **Dark-Mode Modernism**. It prioritizes structured bento grids, hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), frosted glass layers (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`), and specular edge highlights.

## Colors

The core color system is rooted in deep obsidian black (`#0B0C0E`), punctuated by a dynamic chromatic gradient duotone—Aura Violet (`#6E44FF`) to Aura Orange (`#FF5A1F`)—and grounded by razor-sharp monochrome paper and ink tokens.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Canvas & Obsidian Fields (`#0B0C0E`):** The primary ground plane. Creates boundless depth allowing luminous glass elements and specular borders to emerge.
- **Aura Accents (`#6E44FF` & `#FF5A1F`):** Used strictly as luminous background light engines, active state outlines, and subtle typographic spark marks (`✦`). Gradients blend smoothly across linear spans (e.g., `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(110, 68, 255, 0.42), rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.48))`).
- **Paper Whites (`#F6F7F9` / `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headline foregrounds and light inverted bento blocks, creating deliberate editorial contrast against dark sections.
- **Glass Transparency & Specular Highlight Borders:** Semi-translucent dark glass (`rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.66)`) reinforced by light specular edges (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) simulate physical refractive optical lenses.
- **Status Emerald (`#16A34A`):** Dedicated exclusively to the pulsating availability beacon for real-time status signals.

## Typography

Typography functions as both the primary architectural structure and an expressive design layer.

### Typographic Trinity
1. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Primary Grotesk):** Engineered geometry with high x-height and clean curves. Used for structured headings, body copy, and UI controls.
2. **Instrument Serif (Editorial Accent):** Integrated via italic styles at `1.12em` relative scale within headlines and vision statements. Injects editorial elegance directly against geometric sans glyphs.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Telemetry):** Reserved for technical metadata, structural indexing tags (`(01) // INDEX`), timestamps, live Jakarta clocks, and code markers.

### Styling Conventions
- All section indexing labels (`label-mono-sm` and `label-mono-micro`) must be transformed to uppercase with positive tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.1em`).
- Hero titles and section headlines utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`) and clipped line heights (`0.96` to `1.05`) to generate immediate visual tension.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a centered global container with a maximum width of `1320px` and responsive gutters.

### Grid & Bento Ratios
- **Global Container:** `max-width: 1320px; margin: 0 auto; padding: 0 4vw;`
- **Hero Grid (12-Column):**
  - Columns 1–5: Primary editorial title, lead biography, and live telemetry pill.
  - Columns 6–9: Hero visual container / focal refractive window.
  - Columns 10–12: Metric bento stack and preview card.
- **Section Bento Configurations:**
  - Experience / Focus: Asymmetric split `1.35fr : 1fr` (gap: `24px`).
  - Tech & Capability Matrix: 3-column split `1.4fr : 1fr : 1fr` (gap: `22px`).
  - Featured Showcase: Wide-panel split `1.1fr : 1fr` (gap: `36px`).

### Vertical Rhythm
- **Dark Editorial Bands:** `padding: 120px 4vw;` for high-impact transition zones.
- **Standard Bento Sections:** `padding: 100px 0;` to maintain expansive vertical white space.
- **Hero Frame:** `padding-top: 128px; padding-bottom: 56px;` accommodating floating pill navigation.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1025px - 1440px+):** Full 12-column grid and multi-column bento matrices.
- **Tablet (769px - 1024px):** Bento structures collapse to `1fr 1fr` or single-column focus rails. Floating nav hides secondary links and preserves brand and primary CTA.
- **Mobile (<= 768px):** All bento cards reflow to a pure single-column vertical stack with `16px` element gaps and `16px` card padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **specular edge highlights**, **ambient refractive glow filters**, and **multi-layered frosted glass**. Drop shadows are minimized in favor of luminance and edge definition.

### Depth Mechanics
1. **Background Prismatic Auras (Layer 0):** Organic radial gradients positioned behind content surfaces, blurred using large CSS filters (`filter: blur(42px)` to `blur(90px)`). They produce ambient, shifting light cones underneath content panes.
2. **Glassmorphic Substrates (Layer 1):** Dark tinted backdrop surfaces (`rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.66)`) running hardware-accelerated frosted glass filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(140%)`).
3. **Specular Edge Definition:** Cards and pill buttons feature a `1px` high-contrast border on their top and left axes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` specular or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` ambient outline) simulating light hitting beveled glass edges.
4. **Floating Nav & Bar Elevation:** Subtle, deep ambient shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`) combined with frosted backdrops ensure clear visual detachment from scrolling page content.

## Shapes

The design uses a strictly tiered shape hierarchy that correlates curvature with element scale.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` / `999px`):** Used exclusively for interactive actions and small contextual badges: floating navigation bars, status indicators, and primary CTA buttons.
- **Large Bento Containers (`24px` – `26px`):** Used for primary section containers, visual project showcases, and experience bento blocks.
- **Nested Bento Sub-cards (`18px` – `22px`):** Used for secondary cards nested within larger bento layouts.
- **Floating Action Strips & Inner Panels (`14px`):** Reserved for control overlays and inner media containers.
- **Tech Pills & Code Badges (`10px` – `12px`):** Skill tags and monospace indices.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA Pill:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `10px 18px`. Solid white background (`#FFFFFF`) with deep obsidian text (`#111215`). Font is `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (`600`). On hover, scales subtly (`translateY(-2px)`) with an ambient violet-tinted box shadow.
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** Pill-shaped, transparent dark glass fill (`rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.66)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text in paper white (`#F6F7F9`). On hover, border transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.

### Floating Navigation Bar
- Positioned fixed at `top: 18px`, centered with `z-index: 50`.
- Outer pill container features frosted glass (`rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.66)`), hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`, and `padding: 6px`.
- Contains:
  1. Brand avatar/monogram pill.
  2. JetBrains Mono live Jakarta clock (`HH:mm:ss WIB`) accompanied by an emerald live indicator dot.
  3. Floating navigation links with muted text shifting to paper white on hover.
  4. Quick-action contact pill button.

### Bento Cards
- Standard cards feature dark glass backgrounds (`rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.66)`), border radius `24px`, padding `32px`, and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Cards include a technical header: `label-mono-sm` index tag (`(01) // WORK`) alongside geometric title headings.
- On hover, cards lift `4px` (`transform: translateY(-4px)`) via `--ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` with border luminance brightening to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.28)`.

### Status Badges & Telemetry Pills
- Pill-shaped (`999px`), padding `6px 12px`.
- Features an animated pulsating beacon: `8px` emerald dot (`#16A34A`) with an expanding pseudo-element ping animation.
- Text rendered in `JetBrains Mono` at `11px` / `12px` uppercase tracking.

### Tech Skill & Attribute Chips
- Border radius `10px` or `12px`, padding `6px 12px`.
- Filled with dark surface glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) and a delicate border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- On hover, the border shifts to the primary aura violet (`#6E44FF`) with text brightening to paper white.

### Data & Certification Table Rows
- Horizontal list structure with grid template `110px 1fr auto auto` and column gap `24px`.
- Padding `24px 16px` with a bottom hairline divider (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Hovering smoothly highlights the background with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` and reveals external link directional arrows (`↗`).
